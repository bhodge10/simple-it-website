IT Support FAQ for Cincinnati Businesses

You're a Kentucky company. Why should a Cincinnati business hire you?

Because we're 12 minutes away and we compete on the things big Cincinnati MSPs won't: a 15-minute response guarantee in writing, month-to-month contracts, and published pricing. Our Ft. Mitchell office is closer to downtown Cincinnati than many providers based in Cincinnati's own suburbs — the river is not an IT service boundary.

Do you provide onsite support in Cincinnati or only remote?

Both. Most issues are resolved remotely within minutes, but when onsite support is needed, we dispatch technicians the same day. Downtown Cincinnati is approximately 12 minutes from our office, and we serve clients throughout the city including Over-the-Rhine, Clifton, Hyde Park, Oakley, Blue Ash, and the greater Cincinnati area. Same-day onsite service is included in all our managed IT plans.

How much does IT support cost in Cincinnati?

Most of our managed IT clients pay $99–199 per user per month, which typically works out to $1,500–$2,500/month for a business with 5–15 employees and $2,500–$4,500 for 15–30 employees. Unlike most Cincinnati MSPs, we publish our pricing ranges and confirm exact numbers during a free assessment — no mystery quotes.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. Everything we do is month-to-month. Most Cincinnati MSPs require 1–3 year agreements; we think we should earn your business every month instead. Our 98% client retention rate is why we can afford to work this way.

What happens if you miss your 15-minute response guarantee?

We credit that month's service fee for the affected user or device. The guarantee is written into our service agreement — it's a contractual commitment, not a marketing slogan.

Can you support a Cincinnati business with HIPAA or SOC 2 compliance needs?

Yes. We support businesses across multiple compliance frameworks including HIPAA for healthcare, and we help businesses prepare for SOC 2 and other audits with proper access controls, encryption, monitoring, and documentation. Cincinnati's concentration of healthcare and financial services businesses means compliance-ready IT is one of our most in-demand services in the area.

We already have an IT provider. How disruptive is switching?

Less than you'd think — onboarding is our specialty, and we handle the handoff directly with your outgoing provider: documentation, credentials, licensing, and monitoring cutover. Most transitions complete within 2–4 weeks with no downtime for your team, and you don't have to referee between vendors.

What size Cincinnati businesses do you work with?

Our sweet spot is 5–50 employees — the businesses that are too big to run on a computer-savvy office manager but not big enough to justify a full internal IT department. If you have internal IT staff, we also work alongside them, covering monitoring, security, and after-hours support.
